ZapNull — Account & Data Deletion

ZapNull is a business messaging service operated by SOLVIA TECH LIMITED, a company registered in the United Kingdom. This page explains how to request deletion of your account and your data.

Deleting your account

ZapNull is a B2B service. Your account is created and managed by your company's Account Holder or administrator. For security and compliance reasons, individual users cannot delete their own account directly through the app.

How to request account deletion:

Option 1 — Through your company:
Contact your company's Account Holder or administrator. They have access to remove users from your organisation's ZapNull workspace.

Option 2 — Direct request to ZapNull support:
If you cannot reach your company administrator, email us at:

support@zapnull.co.uk

Subject line: "Account Deletion Request — [your registered email]"

Include your registered email or phone number and the company name. We will coordinate with your company administrator and confirm deletion within 30 days.

Deleting your data without deleting your account

You can delete data within the app at any time without removing your account:

  • Delete individual messages — long-press a message → Delete
  • Use auto-delete (vanishing messages) — set conversation policies to delete messages automatically after a chosen time
  • Clear chat history — wipe all messages in a conversation from your device and the server
  • Remove media — delete files, images, and voice notes individually or in bulk

What gets deleted

  • Your user profile (name, email, phone)
  • Your message history (encrypted content removed from servers)
  • Your contacts and group memberships
  • Your device tokens and active login sessions

What we may retain

  • Audit logs (anonymised) for security compliance — kept up to 12 months as required by UK data protection law
  • Billing records if your company is on a paid plan — kept for up to 7 years as required by HMRC
  • Encrypted message copies sent to other users in conversations — those copies are owned by the recipients and follow their own deletion preferences
