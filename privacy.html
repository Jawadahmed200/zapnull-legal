1. Who we are

ZapNull is a business-to-business encrypted messaging service operated by SOLVIA TECH LIMITED, registered in England and Wales. ZapNull provides secure team communication for organisations through mobile apps (Android, iOS) and a web client.

For data protection enquiries, our designated contact is reachable at privacy@zapnull.co.uk.

2. Data we collect

Account and identity data

When your company administrator creates your ZapNull account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Username
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by you)
  • Company name and role within the company

Authentication data

  • Hashed password (we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Hashed recovery password (used to encrypt your end-to-end encryption key backup)
  • Active login sessions and refresh tokens
  • Local device PIN (stored only on your device, never transmitted)

Messaging data

  • Messages, files, images, voice notes, and videos you send
  • Group and conversation memberships
  • Message read receipts and delivery status

All message content is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it leaves. We cannot read your messages — only the intended recipients can decrypt them.

Device and technical data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push token
  • Device model, operating system version, app version
  • IP address (used for security and fraud prevention)
  • Approximate location based on IP (country / region only — never GPS coordinates)

App activity

  • Login and logout events
  • Crash reports (anonymised)
  • Audit logs visible to your company administrator (e.g., login times, device approvals)

We do not collect: precise GPS location, financial information, health data, biometric data, or contacts list from your phone.

3. How we use your data

We use the data described above to:

  • Provide the ZapNull messaging service to you and your organisation
  • Authenticate you and prevent unauthorised access
  • Deliver push notifications for new messages
  • Comply with our legal obligations under UK law
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to security threats and fraud
  • Improve service reliability through anonymised crash reports

We do not sell your personal data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with:

  • Your company administrators — they can see your profile, login activity, and audit metadata for accounts in their organisation. They cannot read encrypted message content.
  • Other ZapNull users in your organisation — when you message them, they receive the encrypted content (which only they can decrypt).
  • Service providers who help us operate the service:
    • Microsoft Azure (UK region) — secure cloud hosting
    • Google Firebase — push notification delivery only (FCM); message content is end-to-end encrypted before reaching Firebase
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only when legally required, and only the data we hold (which excludes message content due to end-to-end encryption)

5. How we protect your data

  • End-to-end encryption — messages, files, voice, and video are encrypted on your device using X25519 + AES-256-GCM before leaving. Only intended recipients can decrypt.
  • Encrypted in transit — all communication with our servers uses TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Encrypted at rest — databases are encrypted on Azure servers
  • Recovery key backup — your private encryption keys are encrypted with your recovery password before being stored. We cannot decrypt them.
  • Access controls — admin actions are audited; production access is restricted to authorised SOLVIA TECH personnel

6. How long we keep your data

  • Active account data — for as long as your account is active
  • Messages — until deleted by you, the recipient, or by your conversation's auto-delete policy
  • Audit logs — up to 12 months for security compliance
  • Billing records (paid plans) — up to 7 years as required by HMRC
  • Anonymised crash reports — up to 90 days

When your account is deleted, all personal data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

7. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion (see our account deletion page)
  • Restrict processing — limit how we use your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@zapnull.co.uk.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Children's privacy

ZapNull is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact privacy@zapnull.co.uk and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. The "Effective date" at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
